How it works

Everything a caregiver needs, in one place

CareCompass provides information only from verified Singapore caregiving resources from our partners.

Watch a quick tutorial

Ask anything

Ask about care, subsidies, or everyday challenges and get clear, plain-language answers, including curated care service recommendations suited to your situation.

Find services

Browse a holistic list of daycare, respite, and home care services, narrowed to what actually fits you, from location to income level.

Match subsidies

From the information you provide, CareCompass identifies subsidies worth applying for, easing the financial burden of caregiving so you don't miss valuable support.

Check in on their wellbeing

CareCompass now also lets you have daily check-ins with your loved one. Simple check-ins help you notice changes in their mood, and alert you early if something seems off.

About us

Caregivers, building for caregivers

We're a volunteer team of primary and secondary caregivers, building CareCompass on top of our full-time jobs. CareCompass began as a winning project at the 2024 Build for Good Hackathon by Open Government Products, shaped by conversations with caregivers across Singapore and guidance from the Agency for Integrated Care and Dementia Singapore.

Selected for the Build for Good Accelerator, we're now growing with our first community partners, and we're just getting started.
