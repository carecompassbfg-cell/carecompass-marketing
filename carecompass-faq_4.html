Frequently asked questions

How CareCompass finds and verifies its information, how we handle your data, and answers to common questions from caregivers.

Trust and how it works
How can we know that the responses are trustworthy?

The responses are collated from, and restricted to, existing resources tied to government bodies or trusted caregiver websites. Some examples are caregiver guides and information from AIC, Dementia Singapore, MOH, SingHealth, Caregivers Alliance, Homage, and Jaga-Me.

For specific guided paths, such as subsidy eligibility or personalised daycare options, we rigorously check all in-built information and paths. CareCompass also incorporates Google reviews of dementia daycare centres and ranks them accordingly in the interface as a first-cut measure of general trustworthiness.

The conversational assistant is fine-tuned using a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) framework on these resources, with in-built heuristics to ensure it prioritises and adheres to content from these trusted datasets.

How does this compare to general information online or tools like ChatGPT?

A typical general chatbot tends to give broad, generic overviews, and lacks suggested prompts, guided steps, and value-added features.

CareCompass provides bite-sized, curated options in a local Singapore context, based on user profiles and tailored to each caregiver's needs and preferences.

Beyond information, CareCompass also offers suggested use areas, such as making caregivers aware of subsidies, actionable steps, such as applying for subsidies, and other value-added features, such as tracking the status of a subsidy application.

The eventual aim is for CareCompass to become a handy "360 co-pilot" for caregivers, assisting them across the many contexts of caregiving.

Does CareCompass give medical, legal, or financial advice?

No. CareCompass is an information and navigation aid to help caregivers find services, subsidies, and resources more easily. It does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for professional consultation.

For decisions about care, health, eligibility, or finances, please verify with the relevant professional, agency, or service provider before acting.

Patients usually aren't eligible for subsidies at dementia daycare centres unless they go through AIC. How does CareCompass connect them to such services?

Currently, some caregivers contact dementia daycare services proactively without going through AIC. Three of our interviewees did, and found the personalised search useful.

We have also spoken with AIC and received initial positive feedback that CareCompass addresses a pressing need for many caregivers. Should AIC become an eventual partner, CareCompass could be integrated as an AIC offering to help streamline the subsidy application process.

Using CareCompass
Is CareCompass free to use?

Yes, completely free. We know caregiving already carries a financial burden, and we want this to be a tool that helps rather than adds to it. CareCompass is funded and supported by our partners, including Open Government Products and better.sg.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can use CareCompass and browse the full catalogue of schemes and daycare and home care services in Singapore without signing up.

Creating an account is optional, and lets you get personalised recommendations and save your searches to your profile.

What areas of Singapore does CareCompass cover?

CareCompass covers services and schemes across Singapore, island-wide.

Privacy and your data
Is my information private and secure?

We take your privacy seriously. We collect only what is needed to provide the service, protect it with appropriate security measures, and we do not sell your personal data.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

What information does CareCompass collect?

Depending on how you use CareCompass, this may include basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, the details you enter to get personalised help such as a care recipient's living situation or household income for subsidy checks, and anything you send us directly such as an email or newsletter sign-up.

The full list, and how each is used, is set out in our Privacy Policy.

Are my conversations used to train AI models?

No. Your conversations are not used to train or fine-tune AI models.

To generate responses, what you type is sent to OpenAI for processing. Chat logs in CareCompass are deleted after 180 days. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

How do I access, correct, or delete my data?

You can access and update your information in your profile at any time. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted with it.

For any other request, or to withdraw consent, contact us at carecompass.bfg@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how CareCompass ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal data when you use our website and application at carecompass.sg and my.carecompass.sg (together, the "Platform"). We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type and operating system, the pages you visit, and the date and time of access;
  • Information you provide to receive personalised help, for example a care recipient's living situation, household income, or care needs entered for subsidy checks and service recommendations;
  • Account information, if you choose to create an account, including your saved searches and bookmarks;
  • Information you send us directly through contact links or when subscribing to our newsletter, such as your email address.

You can use CareCompass without creating an account. An account is optional, and enables personalised recommendations and saved searches.

Information about the person you care for

To give personalised recommendations, you may enter information about the person you care for, such as their living situation, household income, or wellbeing check-ins. Please share only what is needed, and only where you are comfortable doing so on their behalf. You can delete this information at any time from your profile.

How we use your information

We use personal data only for the purposes for which it was collected, including to:

  • Provide and personalise CareCompass's guidance, subsidy matching, and service recommendations;
  • Respond to your enquiries and requests;
  • Understand and improve how the Platform is used;
  • Send you updates or newsletters, where you have chosen to receive them. You may unsubscribe at any time;
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Artificial intelligence

CareCompass uses AI to answer questions and generate recommendations. To do this, the text you enter is sent to OpenAI for processing, which may take place outside Singapore.

Your conversations are not used to train or fine-tune AI models. OpenAI may retain inputs for a limited period for its own operational and safety purposes, in line with its terms as an API provider.

Cookies and analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the Platform is used, and Vercel's analytics and infrastructure to serve and monitor the site, so we can improve CareCompass for caregivers. We also use Sentry to detect and diagnose technical errors. We do not use analytics to identify you personally, and we do not sell your data.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, use, alteration, or disclosure, using industry-standard security technologies and procedures. Access to accounts is managed through authenticated sign-in. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted with it. Chat logs are deleted after 180 days. Where we no longer need data, we delete or anonymise it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that make CareCompass work:

  • OpenAI, to process your questions and generate responses;
  • Clerk, to manage sign-in and account authentication;
  • Vercel, to host the Platform;
  • Amazon Web Services, to host our database;
  • OneMap and Google Maps, to find services near you and estimate travel times;
  • PostHog, to understand how the Platform is used;
  • Sentry, to detect and diagnose technical errors.

Some of these providers process data outside Singapore. We may also disclose data to government authorities or law enforcement where required by law.

Your rights

You have the right to access and correct your personal data, to withdraw consent to its collection, use, or disclosure, and to request its deletion, subject to legal or operational requirements. You can access and update much of your information directly in your profile. For other requests, contact us below.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Policy, wish to exercise your rights, or want to raise a concern about how we handle your data, please contact us at carecompass.bfg@gmail.com.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on the Platform, and where appropriate we will notify you. Please review this Policy periodically.
